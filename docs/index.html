About Bench Press

Bench Press turns 103 journalism codes of ethics and related normative documents into 919 code-specific value tensions, each tested with up to four minimal pairs of news passages that differ in one value-relevant editorial choice. A model's answer is scored against the code's own prescription, not against a universal ethical answer. Four open-weight models were evaluated in five prompting configurations, from no framing (C0) to the code's policy text and scenarios (C4), with and without a written rationale.

Reading the numbers

  • Accuracy is the share of parsed answers choosing the passage the code supports; 50% is chance.
  • Unless a table says otherwise, results are from the arm without a rationale request, with the gold audit applied. Confidence intervals in the tables are 95% cluster-bootstrap intervals over code × tension cells.
  • The explorer shows point estimates computed from the counts in data/explorer.js; per-code values rest on few answers and should be read as descriptive.
  • Items numbered S1, S2, … are referred to in the paper but printed only here.

Data and code

The benchmark data, the evaluation runner and the scorer are in this repository (see its README). The dashboard's data are aggregates: counts of code-aligned answers per cell, model and configuration. No individual model answers or rationales are published. Data: CC BY 4.0; code: MIT.
